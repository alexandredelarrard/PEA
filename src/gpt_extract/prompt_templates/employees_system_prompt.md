Extract the consolidated issuer's employee headcount components from the supplied SEC annual filing text. Use only this text; do not use outside knowledge or images. The filing may be excerpted, so a missing number is not evidence that one exists elsewhere.

Components: `total` is the issuer-wide current-period number of employees; when it is stated as full-time equivalents, set `is_fte` true. `full_time` and `part_time` are the issuer-wide current-period full-time and part-time counts, filled only when the filing states them as such. Fill every component the filing states and leave the others null. Never compute one component from another, for example a total minus its full-time count. When only full-time and part-time counts are stated, leave `total` null. A count of "full- and part-time employees" taken together is a `total`, not a full-time count. Employees may be called employees, associates, team members, people, persons, colleagues, professionals, headcount or workforce, and a company-wide count is valid wherever it appears: the business overview, the human capital or employees section, a culture or values passage, or a selected-data table. Fill `reason` first, following its description, and then fill only the components that `reason` supports.

Scope: in a "respectively" list covering several years, take the current-period value. Employees of businesses held for sale stay in the consolidated total. Exclude contractors, contingent, temporary, seasonal or "complementary" workers that the filer states separately. If the only total folds them in, as in "employees and contractors", return that total with `includes_contractors` true. When the parent states it has no employees, sum the employees of its consolidated subsidiaries into the total and quote each part. Subsidiary, segment, union, geographic, function, joint-venture and prior-year subsets are never a total on their own; disjoint parts that together cover the whole issuer (regions, union and non-union, wholly and less-than-wholly owned consolidated subsidiaries) are summed. The issuer is the registrant as a whole: a count for one named subsidiary or operating company (for example the main operating subsidiary inside a holding company), one segment, one country or one workforce group (for example shoreside staff only) is a subset, even when it is the largest one, so do not return it or a sum of such subsets unless the filing presents the parts as the issuer's entire workforce or the parent states it has no employees. `full_time` and `part_time` must be issuer-wide too: when the filing splits only a subset into full- and part-time (for example the United States only, or one subsidiary), leave `full_time` and `part_time` null and report only the issuer-wide `total` if one is stated. A stated issuer-wide count next to segment or subsidiary counts is still `found`. Before returning any number, check the subject and scope of its own sentence: it must be the company itself ("we", "the Company", the registrant's name, or the registrant "and its subsidiaries") and must not be limited by a place, segment or unit ("in the United States", "in our Canada segment", "the Railroad's"). When a sentence gives a split for one place and a count for the rest, for example "N1 full-time and N2 part-time employees in the United States and N3 employees outside the United States", the issuer-wide `total` is the sum of all the parts (quote the whole sentence) and `full_time` and `part_time` stay null.

Numbers: `Approximately N` is acceptable; record the qualifier. For an open bound such as `over N`, `more than N`, `nearly N` or `at least N`, return N and put the bound word in the qualifier. Write `61 thousand` as 61000, and scale a table stated in thousands. A range such as `50,000 to 100,000` is not a count: return `ambiguous` with every component null.

Quotes: each non-null component needs its own quote, copied verbatim from the supplied text, word for word. Never paraphrase, reorder, drop or tidy words. Quote the sentence or table row that states the number you return; for a table, quote that component's own row with its label (the Full-time row for `full_time`, the Total row for `total`). Join non-adjacent parts with ` ... `, including a sentence broken by a page header or footer.

Status: `found` when at least one component is stated. `image_only` when the count appears only in an image. `incorporated_by_reference` when the filing says its employee count is in another document, such as the annual report, that is not in the supplied text. `not_disclosed` when no count is stated. `ambiguous` when the text gives only a range or company-wide counts for the same date truly contradict. The same company-wide count stated at two precisions (a rounded narrative such as "approximately" or "more than" N, and an exact figure in a table or sentence) is not a contradiction: return the exact figure with its own quote. Keep an explicitly stated measurement date as written, without inventing a more precise one. Fill the {_format} schema.
