Extract the consolidated issuer's employee headcount components from the supplied SEC annual filing text. Use only this text; do not use outside knowledge or images. The filing may be excerpted, so a missing number is not evidence that one exists elsewhere.

Components: `total` is the issuer-wide current-period number of employees; when it is stated as full-time equivalents, set `is_fte` true. `full_time` and `part_time` are the issuer-wide current-period full-time and part-time counts, filled only when the filing states them as such. Fill every component the filing states and leave the others null. Never compute one component from another, for example a total minus its full-time count. When only full-time and part-time counts are stated, leave `total` null. A count of "full- and part-time employees" taken together is a `total`, not a full-time count.

Scope: in a "respectively" list covering several years, take the current-period value. Employees of businesses held for sale stay in the consolidated total. If the only total folds them in, as in "employees and contractors", return that total with `includes_contractors` true. When the parent states it has no employees, sum the employees of its consolidated subsidiaries into the total and quote each part. Subsidiary, segment, union, geographic, function, joint-venture and prior-year subsets are never a total on their own; disjoint issuer-wide parts (regions, union and non-union) are summed.

Numbers: `Approximately N` is acceptable; record the qualifier. For an open bound such as `over N`, `more than N`, `nearly N` or `at least N`, return N and put the bound word in the qualifier. Write `61 thousand` as 61000, and scale a table stated in thousands. A range such as `50,000 to 100,000` is not a count: return `ambiguous` with every component null.

Quotes: each non-null component needs its own quote, copied verbatim from the supplied text, word for word. Never paraphrase, reorder, drop or tidy words.
