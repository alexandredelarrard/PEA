Written 2026-10-02. Branch harness/earnings-call-extraction-gaps @ 166a9f2 (cleaned text, P4b).

WHAT IT RUNS
  The production stage `score_earnings_calls(context)` (src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_features.py),
  i.e. exactly what `build-text` runs first, but WITHOUT the OpenAI pass and WITHOUT the cube build.
  Driver: <run>/_scripts/p5_score_only.py (throwaway; builds the context with get_config_context and
  calls the production function; no product code). It splits + cleans every call (split_call + the
  P4b cleaner), applies the quality gate, scores the two sections `prepared_remarks` (management
  prepared text) and `qa` (management ANSWERS only) with yiyanghkust/finbert-tone, and upserts ONE
  TICKER AT A TIME into `earnings_call_sentiment` under model tag
      yiyanghkust/finbert-tone:speaker-clean-v1
  The 67 probe calls under the OLD tag `...:speaker-v1` are ignored and rescored.

RESUME IF INTERRUPTED
  Just run the same command again. The cache is incremental: the remaining work is
  (stored calls x {prepared_remarks, qa}) minus the keys already saved under the speaker-clean-v1 tag,
  and rows are saved per ticker, so at most the ticker in flight is redone. A finished run logs
  "Earnings-call sentiment: +N newly scored rows"; a re-run after completion re-splits only the
  ~436 gate-failing calls (they are never scored, by design) and scores nothing.
  Do not run two scoring processes at once (they would score the same tickers twice).
