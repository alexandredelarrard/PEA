# Cube aggregation

## Summary

`src/data_aggregate` converts normalized extract tables into a wide modelling cube. Eight domain sub-steps persist nine registered parts. The [assembler](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_assemble_cube.py) merges the YAML-listed parts onto the full persisted price grid (root: the price part), preserves latest rows with null targets for prediction, and checks output schema and row count.

## Responsibilities

- Normalize prices and define the trading grid.
- Build forward targets and rolling factor loadings.
- Build fundamental, momentum, text, institutional, and governance feature panels.
- Apply peer-relative and cross-sectional transformations without future leakage, preserving binary states and structural zeros.
- Match financial changes by fiscal period, enforce publication-time visibility, and expire stale point-in-time values by source cadence.
- Recompute guarded trailing windows and detect schema drift.
- Refuse missing compulsory or broken populated inputs; skip absent/empty configured optional parts. Readiness names required refreshes against the analysis universe's latest stored source-price date through [part_status.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/part_status.py).
- Stream the final cube to the store without materializing all output chunks twice.

## Price-part contract

[StepCubePrices](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_cube_prices.py) loads projected full-history source evidence once, cleans Yahoo quote/total repairs and volume units, resolves each ticker's actions and share observations through its owner windows (`price_owner_windows`; a ticker with no row is its own owner), computes the level history, the PIT outputs and the forward basis, cleans returns, checks the complete candidate, then writes one part. Yahoo supplies prices; Sharadar supplies reconciliation and share/action evidence. No Sharadar DAILY market-cap reader is introduced.

Cross-package corporate-action, quantity and capitalization mathematics live in [src/utils/price](../../src/utils/price/levels.py); aggregation-only loading, cleaning, projection and checks live in [data_aggregate/utils/price](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/price/inputs.py). Shared conversion retains explicit source/economic dates and ordinary-per-security units. Ordinary ShareBasis.rebase semantics remain unchanged.

The existing unmanaged cube_part_prices persists float64 close_pit (historical quoted security close), shares_outstanding_pit (latest public source count in that session’s canonical security units, times the supported vendor class coefficient: canonical-class equivalents, so BRK-A counts as 1,500 BRK-B), and mcap_pit = close_pit × shares_outstanding_pit (issuer USD capitalization). The coefficient is 1 for every single-class issuer and for multi-class issuers whose classes trade one-for-one. Nonunit coefficients are matched by ticker/public date/fiscal period and vendor count and are public from that public date. Supersession ends support even when the successor is unsupported. Unknown units or unsupported observations stay null. Cached original counts are not independent unit evidence. Matching raw observations are restored with their own representation-vintage factor F_rep(s,v)=F_current(s)/F_current(v), not a ticker-wide timestamp or nominal price factor. Unmatched counts require corroborated originals or an identity with no future nonunit vendor events. Unknown selected predecessor ownership remains null. Raw history stays unchanged. Two more float64 columns carry the quantity basis: `security_basis` F(d), the forward product of the genuine splits and the `quote_units` change dated at or before d (`q_t = q_s · F_t / F_s`), and `volume_pit`, vendor volume × F_d/F_T where the Yahoo tape restatement after d equals F_T/F_d (`compatible_tape_support`: after `terminal_tape` the saved tape differs from raw traded shares only by the genuine events Yahoo's own split ledger also dates; the spin-off price factors it files as splits are undone first), else null. Neither enters the assembled cube.

close_split and close_total retain their adjusted meanings; returns continue to use close_total. The persisted level_factor is dimensionless, S = Y × H / (F × U). Historical quotes are close_split × S × F × U. No return is calculated from close_pit.

Schema changes promote to a full build before payload loading. Incremental runs recompute the full required history and stream the saved rows dated before the refresh window once ([changed_tickers](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/incremental.py)). Every ticker with a missing, extra or differing old row (a split, spin-off, config fix or vendor restatement, or a peer whose `sector_ret` moved) is deleted and rewritten whole; the other tickers get the normal tail refresh; both happen in one store transaction. `build-prices -t X,Y -F` forces the same per-ticker rebuild, `-F` alone is a full rebuild, and `-t` without `-F` is refused. No other part is cleared or rerun.

Governance, institutionals, 13F and the portfolio inputs read only the saved PIT columns: a quantity converts by `convert_quantities` (`q · F_to / F_from`, [levels.py](../../src/utils/price/levels.py)) over the saved `security_basis`, looked up with `lookup_asof` (the last grid date at or before the date; a null there stays null) in [quotes.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/quotes.py). Dated source rows (FINRA, FTD, insider positions) go to each ticker's last saved units with `terminal_rows` over `terminal_basis_factor` (`F_T / F_d`, computed once per institutional build; a null F_d stays null). The ordinary saved-price adapters need no split, unit or owner evidence. The FTD security-line adapter separately consumes its declared full split/action/fundamental-count evidence to establish the source quantity basis. Target size reads the saved `mcap_pit`. Fundamentals and intrinsic value (parked) still go through the shared quantity adapters, which need source dates and genuine G/U evidence to compare observations across dates. Sparse evidence loads once without rerunning price-anchor resolution or repairs. [Source observation proofs](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/price/observations.py) bind financial count/weighted/EPS/DPS comparisons to raw public/fiscal keys, compatible ownership and coherent four-quarter vintages. Supported fields use a normalized working view; mixed, incomplete or unbound windows stay null. Original point/weighted definitions and raw dividend cash reconciliation remain separate. Governance keeps full count history for proxy cleaning when its daily feature grid is bounded.

Momentum retains adjusted returns/candles and independent saved-price seam checks. Its dollar volume is `close_pit × volume_pit`, null unless the quote is positive and the tape non-negative; the tape compatibility proof lives in the price build; `rel_volume_5_63`, `volume_trend_63`, `volume_cv_63` and `signed_vol_63` are computed on that same as-traded dollar volume (not share volume), so a split or spin-off unit step creates no step in them, and they are null wherever the dollar volume is. Archive EPS retains its matched adjusted-price/published-factor pair under source-date and daily unit gates; Yahoo dividend yield retains its matched dividend/adjusted-close pair. Unknown saved counts or issuer caps never gain a local fallback. Detailed canonical-price reconciliation/repair outcomes belong to price creation. Secondary-class tape outside the saved canonical price universe still receives its existing source-specific volume correction once in the institutional input stage. Feature builders retain their own source-support, seam and population diagnostics.

## Earnings-call feature contract

[StepCubeText.run](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_cube_text.py) visibly loads the calendar, plans work, prepares selected text once, runs sentiments, runs embeddings, then builds/persists features. Sentiment is read once during planning and updated in memory with successfully saved scores. Feature creation reuses that frame, loads price frames once, streams saved embeddings, calculates call/daily features, and saves the panel once. Each model stage saves completed calls independently; the existing sentiment and embedding tables remain the reusable outputs.

Planning compares compact ticker/quarter/date keys from paragraph 1 of earnings_call_sections with each model table's compatible completion metadata. Each model has its own missing/failed/stale work set across source history, including old holes. Preparation reads the union's projected paragraph payload once, then loops by ticker/quarter to split, clean and assess. These stages use ordinary DataFrames, with no generator or per-ticker table reads. Stored embedding dates are normalized explicitly as ISO8601 before completion checks and feature joins.

The canonical [parser](../../src/utils/text/earnings_call_split.py), introduction matching and [tokenization/quality checks](../../src/utils/text/text_metrics.py) are shared with extraction/validation. Cube-only [sentiment inference](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/nlp_sentiment.py) and [preparation/persistence helpers](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_preparation.py) belong to aggregation. [Embedding helpers](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_embeddings.py) validate vectors and derive coherence/drift; they do not create sections.

Same-speaker paragraphs merge before Q&A detection. Keep the 150 management-word and 300 question-word rules on merged turns. Before splitting, prepare_sections calls clean_earnings_call_paragraphs to discard provider metadata and participant rosters wherever they occur and remove balanced parenthetical text, including nested annotations. Financial speech is retained regardless of paragraph position. Operator handoffs supply structural cues and never become model text. Empty cleaned paragraphs are dropped; preparation logs exclusions and the paragraphs/characters affected by parentheses. Boilerplate/courtesy cleaning retains hedges and decimal/abbreviation boundaries. The read-only validator uses the same paragraph cleaner.

A dated eligible call needs at least 100 combined cleaned prepared/management-answer words; analyst words do not count toward that floor. Prepared-only and answer-only calls qualify. Sentiment scores prepared remarks, substantive analyst questions and management answers separately. The parser exposes all substantive questions, including short questions below the unchanged ten-word embedding floor. Embeddings preserve their existing turn population and question/answer exchange mapping.

A completed sentiment call has exactly three canonical tags: `prepared_remarks`, `questions`, `answers`. True absence records zero words and NULL probabilities. Existing text with failed/missing inference stays pending; valid partner roles remain usable for their own features. Matching saved prepared scores and legacy management-only `qa` scores can be reused; valid canonical answers take precedence. Canonical rows are saved before scoped legacy-tag deletion, so leftover `qa` never creates false completion or overrides answers. Embeddings validate/save complete actual turn sequences and reconcile stale tails. Optional engine/API unavailability leaves work retryable.

Model choices come from data_aggregate.earnings_calls.sentiment_model and the existing gpt.embedding model/batch_size/max_chars settings. The step validates them at initialization and loads one local sentiment engine only for missing nonempty documents. Model identities include speaker-clean-v3. No global model/factory cache or configurable tag input is used; the canonical sentiment roles have one declaration in the preparation/persistence owner.

Text-step functions and their direct text/parser dependencies keep at most five explicit inputs, roughly 100 lines and three nested control blocks. Short focus comments separate loading, validation, calculation and persistence. Cache validation happens once during planning; newly inferred distributions/vectors are validated before saving. Feature helpers reuse canonical numeric sentiment and identified complete embedding calls without repeating those checks. Fixed parser/FinBERT settings stay with their owning function; unused parser entry points and audit-only parameters are removed.

The output is exactly ten `f_ec_*` columns: three role tone levels, three role deltas, three embedding distances and Q&A coherence. Dictionary uncertainty, combined tone, Q&A tone gap, length delta and issuer-history z-scores are removed. Missing roles leave only their dependent fields NULL; a prepared delta remains valid when Q&A coverage changes.

Normal feature writes start inclusively at the stored frontier minus the configured seven calendar days. Saved score/vector reads retain active emitters and their actual immediately preceding fiscal quarters, including older symbols. Vectors use a projected bounded stream through [DataStore.iter_load](../../src/data_store/store.py); [embedding mathematics](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_embeddings.py) carries unfinished calls across chunks, computes complete-exchange coherence and retains pooled numeric call vectors. The full calendar preserves original positions while daily arrays cover the output window. Call availability uses the earnings-call source policy in [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml), currently call date +3 calendar days rolled to a market session; signal expiry uses the configured trading-session horizon.

Extraction invalidates saved model outputs when a source call changes. Corrections/deletions/backfills outside the normal tail require explicit build-text -F and cube assembly. Full/cold/schema-change feature rebuilding reuses compatible inference. No durable repair marker or cross-stage acknowledgement protocol is used.

### Exact ten earnings-call features

For each role `r`, `T(r,q) = P(positive) - P(negative)` from its cleaned FinBERT document. The existing engine combines nonoverlapping token windows by token count; no positive/negative word dictionary is involved. `q-1` means the same issuer's immediately preceding fiscal quarter.

The formulas live in [sentiment/feature mathematics](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_features.py) and [embedding mathematics](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/text/earnings_call_embeddings.py).

| Persisted feature | Exact per-call calculation |
| --- | --- |
| `f_ec_prep_tone` | `T(prepared,q)`. |
| `f_ec_prep_tone_delta` | `T(prepared,q) - T(prepared,q-1)`. |
| `f_ec_questions_tone` | `T(questions,q)`, using all substantive cleaned analyst questions. |
| `f_ec_questions_tone_delta` | `T(questions,q) - T(questions,q-1)`. |
| `f_ec_answers_tone` | `T(answers,q)`, using management answers. |
| `f_ec_answers_tone_delta` | `T(answers,q) - T(answers,q-1)`. |
| `f_ec_qa_qq_distance` | `1 - cosine(mean Q&A vector_q, mean Q&A vector_q-1)`; equal pooling of retained analyst-question and management-answer turns. |
| `f_ec_prep_qq_distance` | The same cosine distance on retained prepared turns. |
| `f_ec_questions_only_qq_distance` | The same cosine distance on existing retained analyst-question vectors only; no additional embeddings. |
| `f_ec_qa_coherence_mean` | Mean-pool each mapped exchange's question vectors, average cosine to its individual answer vectors, then equally average exchange scores; at least two usable exchanges. |

Each role delta requires a valid current and immediately preceding fiscal-quarter score with compatible provenance; it cannot bridge missing quarters or missing role support. Supported issuer aliases remain comparable. Distances require compatible model identity/vector dimension and that same fiscal adjacency; distances/coherence retain six-decimal rounding. Levels/coherence lie in [-1,1], deltas in [-2,2], distances in [0,2]. Dates, issuer identity, price availability and the original 66-session expiry still apply.

A read-only 2026-10-22 follow-up on 253 stored calls/19,210 paragraphs (2005–2026, eight issuers) plus 19 hand-labelled fixtures across 18 issuers removed 275 stored metadata/roster paragraphs and parenthetical content in 239 retained paragraphs. Text changed in 144 stored calls; eligibility (251 accepted), split status and mapped exchange counts did not change. All 19 fixture outputs stayed identical. The public preparation method made one paragraph read, and all accepted call word/turn counts agreed with the shared read-only validator. The focused suite passed 194 tests with zero live-guard attempts. Local ignored evidence: `reports/validate/2026-10-22-text-preparation-cleaning/after.json`, `pytest.xml` and `validation.md`.

The speaker-clean-v3 cleaning change invalidates saved v2 model compatibility. For the ten-column feature contract, run a full text-part rebuild, then cube assembly; compatible v3 prepared/answer scores and vectors are reused, and only missing question sentiment is added. Existing fitted models require fresh training against the new inputs. Fresh DDL omits uncertainty; an existing unused nullable sentiment column may remain until database recreation. No DataStore API/PK change or automatic database-column drop is required.

## Feature legs: one definition each

Every cube part that ships `_vs_peers`, `_xs` or `_vs_hist` columns builds them through `build_peer_relative_panel` in [common/panel.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/panel.py), which calls the shared helpers in [common/xs.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/xs.py). All z-scores share the one arithmetic `xs.zscore(x, centre, scale, clip)`; the callers keep their own zero-dispersion guards. The presets below are the only definitions; the per-part sections do not restate them.

| Leg | Function | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_vs_peers` | `panel.peer_relative(field_df, peer_dict, std_floor_ratio=0.5)` | per date, `(X - weighted peer mean) / max(weighted peer std, 0.5 x universe std)`; the ticker is never in its own basket; NaN with fewer than three peers reporting or when the peers share one value; inputs used as they are (no trim, no winsorization); a non-finite score becomes NaN, then the score is clipped at `Z_CLIP` = 5 |
| `_vs_hist` | `xs.self_history_z` | per ticker, `(x - trailing mean) / trailing sample std` over the trailing 1,260 sessions ending today, first emitted after 252 sessions, NaN where the std is 0, clipped at `Z_CLIP` = 5; no per-day winsorization, so one raw value gives the same z on every day. Short-flow uses its own window (252, minimum 126) |
| `_xs` | `xs.xs_rank_pct` | per date percentile rank across the universe in [0, 1] (average ties); no clip needed |
| universe z | `xs.xs_z(df, clip)` | per date `(x - mean) / std`; `clip` is a required argument. Momentum `zscore` mode and the target path use it. Characteristic z (factor exposures and the neutralization design): `Z_CLIP` = 5. Modelling label: `XS_CLIP_LABEL` = 3 (unchanged, a different job) |
| change versus prior year or quarter | `pit.fiscal_change_values` and `fiscal_change_to_daily` | the one fiscal-pairing engine (predecessor by fiscal end within 45 days, filing-count fallback when `fiscal_end` is absent, keyed on the later filing); growth base per field: `signed_pct` for netIncome, freeCashflow and operatingIncome, `pct` otherwise (asserted by `test_growth_kind_table.py`) |
| dividend growth | `dividend_features._ttm_dividends`, `_calendar_prior`, `_cagr` | trailing-twelve-month dividends over a 365-calendar-day window divided by the same value on the last session at or before `d - 365` days; the 5-year CAGR uses 5 x 365 days; NaN until a full window of history exists. Levels (TTM, payer, yield, payout, coverage, buyback yield) keep 252 sessions |
| CEO pay growth gate | `governance.panel._ceo_pay_growth` with `pay_features.prior_annual_leg` | the previous total must come from a filing 250 to 550 days earlier; otherwise the cross-filing growth is NaN (the filing's own Summary Compensation Table pair still wins when present) |
| source availability | Per-table `pit` policy in [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml) | Filing-derived sources use1 calendar day; 13F uses filing/public date +2, calls use call date +3, short volume +1, and FTD half-month end +21. The resolver rolls to a market session. Notes retain their configured monthly/quarterly release rule and later observed availability. |

## Governance cleaning stage

`StepCubeGovernance` loads the raw governance tables (`def14a_llm`, `def14a_executive_comp`, `def14a_directors`, `def14a_director_comp`, `sec_8k_votes`, and `def14a_ownership` projected to ticker, accession, holder type and shares). It cleans them once with `clean_governance_inputs` in [clean.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/governance/clean.py). `build_governance_feature_panel` only builds features from the cleaned bundle. No impute, carry, repair or reconcile helper is called anywhere else; `test_governance_clean_stage.py` guards this.

The stage runs in a fixed order: child fills, board merge, `impute_def14a` (carries, identities, CEO stints), board provenance, ownership basis repair, economic ownership, ownership estimate, same-filing SCT pay growth, historical CEO pay backfill, say-on-pay vote fallback. A cleaned bundle is returned unchanged on a second call.

- **Provenance.** Every proxy column the stage writes carries `<col>_source_as_of` (the filing the value comes from) and `<col>_fill`: `filed`, `identity`, `carry`, `person`, `cross_source`, `estimate`, `backfill` or `interp`. A fill that uses a later filing also carries `<col>_evidence_as_of`. `staleness.expire_stale` ages annual observations by `_source_as_of`: they remain current through day 395 (365 + 30 calendar days) after their true source and are unknown from day 396. Carries in the cleaning stage use the same cap and cannot restart this clock. The policy is declared by `GOVERNANCE_ANNUAL_MAX_AGE_DAYS` in [constants.py](../../src/constants/constants.py).
- **CEO stints.** `names.ceo_stint_ids` splits a ticker's proxies on a change of CEO, keyed on the first-listed name with honorifics (Dr., Mr., Prof., ...) stripped; a generational-suffix change (Jr., Sr., II, III) splits only together with a change of first name (WRB father and son), a bare surname is an unknown CEO, and one off-cycle proxy naming a CEO seen nowhere else in the ticker (another company's proxy) belongs to no stint. A co-CEO who becomes the first-listed CEO opens his own stint. `person_key` is unchanged. `ceo_since_year` takes the value at or before the stint's first proxy year that the most filings of the stint disclose (ties go to the largest), else stays as filed. `ceo_is_founder` takes the stint's mode. Both are kind `person`, dated at the row's own `as_of`, and keep the filed value in `<col>_raw`. `ceo_is_board_chair` is carried only within a stint.
- **Ownership estimate.** A NULL `insider_ownership_pct` on a ticker never flagged dual-class is the sum of `director_officer` shares in `def14a_ownership` over point-in-time `sharesOutstandingPit`. Holder rows whose name reads as an entity (Inc, LLC, L.P., Trust, Fund, Vanguard, BlackRock, State Street, a board-held block such as MetLife's Policyholder Trust, ...) are not summed: the extraction tags some 5% holders `director_officer`. Words that also appear in executive titles (Group, Holdings, Capital, Management, Investments, Securities, so also the "as a group" total) are tested on the name before its first comma only. It needs at least 5 remaining holders, a result in (0, 1] and at most 3x the ticker's largest filed value, and is kind `estimate`. Filed, computed and carried values are never overwritten.
- **Pay ratio, board size, tenures.** `ceo_pay_ratio` is CEO total pay over the mean `median_employee_pay` of the last three proxies that carry one (the current one included, one is enough, a proxy older than 1,460 days is ignored); it is blank when the total or every median is missing, and the disclosed ratio is not a fallback. `board_size` is the proxy's roster row count in `def14a_directors` where roster rows exist. `avg_board_tenure` is supplied from the director rows only where the filing's value is missing. A start year disclosed by any proxy of a run of the same audit firm fills the whole run (`auditor_tenure`), and a run that never discloses it stays blank.
- **Provision and dissent features.** A provision with two directions ships as one signed column named after it (`classified_board_change`, `majority_voting_change`, `ceo_board_chair_change`, `independent_chair_change`, `lead_independent_director_change`): +1 when its value goes 0 to 1 between two proxies that state it, -1 for 1 to 0, 0 when unchanged, blank before a second statement and 395 days after the filing. The two governance counts still sum the underlying directions, `dual_class_added` stays a 0/1 event and the poison-pill flag is gone. No longer shipped: `net_governance_change`, the ten-point independence-drop flag, the say-on-pay excess and threshold flags, the board dissent median and its change, the share of nominees under 70% support, the 5% auditor-dissent flag and the four `pay_up_*_down` legs. New: `youngest_director_age` (minimum age, anchored per person) and `f_female_board_chg_pct` (female share minus the mean of the previous three proxies' shares, at least one required).
- **Say-on-pay.** `f_say_on_pay_support` is gone; the 8-K vote series is the single say-on-pay source. A proxy with a filed support value and no 8-K tally in the prior 450 days adds one vote row (for = support, against = 1 − support, `source = proxy`), dated at the proxy.
- **Invalidated measurements.** `measurement_to_daily` and `measurement_change_to_daily` in [staleness.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/governance/staleness.py) preserve rejected observations as carry barriers. A CEO transition, rejected annual comparison, undefined director statistic or unreadable replacement vote ends the previous measurement immediately; another valid observation reopens it. Raw disclosure silence can still carry within the original source's 395 days. CEO-specific levels also end at a confirmed CEO stint change.
- **Vote freshness.** Say-on-pay dissent and director-election dissent use 395 days too. The [SEC permits one-, two- or three-year say-on-pay cycles](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2011/33-9178-secg.htm); the separate frequency vote is at least every six years. The bounded 2011–2026 source audit found 6,793 usable say-on-pay meetings across 488 tickers: among 6,258 consecutive intervals of at least 200 days, the median was 364 days, the 95th percentile 393 days, and 95.2% were at most 395 days. This annual default does not infer cadence from future meetings or treat the advisory frequency preference as adopted policy. Companies voting every two or three years therefore have deliberate unknown periods after day 395. Local evidence: `reports/validate/2026-10-15-governance-freshness/cadence.json`.
- **SCT pay growth.** `ceo_comp_growth_sct` is log(total FY / total FY−1) from the CEO's row in the filing's own Summary Compensation Table, filer-stated totals only. `ceo_pay_growth` and the internal `ceo_comp_growth_1y` (the pay leg of the pay-versus-performance fields, built but not shipped as a column) prefer it and fall back to the cross-filing pair; the CEO-change mask still applies. The cross-filing pair counts only when the previous total comes from a filing 250 to 550 days earlier (`prior_annual_leg`), so a skipped year gives NaN rather than a two-year change labelled one-year growth. `ceo_pay_growth` is that log growth clipped to ±`PAY_LOG_GROWTH_BOUND` (3, [panel.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/governance/panel.py)), identical on every date with no cross-sectional trim, so one raw growth always ships as one value; `ceo_pay_vs_revenue_growth` is pay log growth minus revenue log growth, clipped to the same bound. A NULL parent `ceo_total_comp` takes the CEO's same-year SCT total (kind `identity`).
- **Governance peer legs.** The twelve `_vs_peers` columns (board size, CEO pay ratio, CEO equity pay share, insider ownership, director equity pay share and seven vote-dissent legs) use the one shared `peer_relative` in [common/panel.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/panel.py), like every other cube part: `(X - weighted peer mean) / max(weighted peer std, 0.5 x universe std)` per date, NaN when fewer than three peers report, no input trim and no winsorization, clipped at `Z_CLIP` = 5 (see [Feature legs](#feature-legs-one-definition-each)). `ceo_pay_ratio` is log1p-transformed first; insider ownership and the dissent shares use the square root; the other fields are used as is. The two pay-misalignment fields are the difference of two such scores, each clipped at 5, so they span +-10 and ship raw.

> ⚠ **Historical pay backfill is a deliberate lookahead.** A NULL CEO pay component or total is filled from a LATER proxy's SCT for the same CEO and fiscal year (`backfill`), or log-linearly inside one stint across at most 2 missing proxies (`interp`). In live data a value missing today is not filled this way, so history is more complete than the present. The step logs the counts as one WARNING. The lookahead is concentrated in early history: the share of proxies with at least one pay cell from a later filing is 35-54% in 2000-2005, 15-20% in 2006-2011 and 4-7% from 2012 (0 on any ticker's latest proxy), so a backtest on 2000-2011 sees denser pay history than live data and a train/test split straddling 2006 mixes the regimes. Two smaller lookaheads are also accepted: stint `person` values come from the stint's other filings, and the estimate's "ever dual-class" test and its filed-value ceiling read later proxies (they can only refuse a fill). An exact later SCT value also replaces a carried salary, and a bridged (`interp`) total feeds no cross-filing growth and is flagged `comp_imputed`.

> ⚠ **Run `build-governance -F` for corrections older than the configured write tail, including after proxy season when needed.** Original later-filing stint/pay fills remain in place. Normal runs rewrite 380 calendar days, bounded by the table's configured start/end in [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml). Older rows retain their last-built values until an explicit full governance rebuild and subsequent cube assembly.

Every rule's count is logged as `governance clean | <stage>|<rule>: n`; `test_clean_stage_tally_keys` pins the stage names. Evidence: `reports/validate/2026-10-10-governance-fill-review/`.

## SEC 13F values and immutable books

The approved institutional code is merged into `dev` at `27e4f723f2e61a4dffd54d11f4785cee89e2b9ee`; its reviewed worktree remains at `reports/worktrees/institutional-13f-pit`. The manager profile uses the shared [value repair](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/sec_thirteen/value_basis.py): one supported monetary factor per manager/report-quarter/accession, checked against report-quarter-end as-traded prices. The unit is the modal power of ten among at least five priced securities and is accepted only when 80% of them land inside the row band (0.35-3.5 times the quote); otherwise it stays 1 and the filing is `abstained`, never rejected. After the unit is frozen each common line is checked once: inside the band it is kept; outside it (or when value equals shares) the corrupt leg is rebuilt, value = shares x quote while that stays under the issuer's market cap and the cover total, else shares = value / quote while that stays under shares outstanding, else the line is dropped. Securities the price table does not cover are priced by a consensus (median value / shares per period and CUSIP over at least three roster filers, built in one pre-pass). A missing value is rebuilt from the quote or the line stays unknown; one bad line never voids the book. A book is accepted when at least 80% of its common lines are kept and 80% of its cover total is retained; a rejected book only loses its weights. One ceiling rule then excludes a common line above 110% of its issuer's market cap (or $5tn) and, for a book still above $12tn, its largest lines; nothing raises. Filings with a report period before 1999 or after today are dropped and bond (`debt`) lines leave the book, the manifest row count reduced to match. Estimates do not vote for a unit factor. Call/put legs use their own underlying quantities. Quotes are nonfuture and at most seven calendar days old. Logs distinguish scaling, estimates, exclusions and unresolved prices.

Only [`sec13f_hr`](../reference/table-catalog.md#existing-table-13f-versions--approved-storage-correction) stores source versions; the manager profile and the cluster features both read it. Positions and one hidden `cusip=_empty` completion record commit atomically per accession. Originals and amendments coexist; legacy rows remain unverified `_legacy` accessions. Source-complete empty books are observable. No auxiliary filing table or JSON data store is required.

The cluster features read originals only: a filing counts when it is filed by the quarter's deadline (`period + 45` days rolled to the next trading day, `cluster_panel.filing_deadlines`), amendments are ignored, and a quarter is published on the first trading day on or after that deadline plus the `sec13f_hr` publication lag of [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml) (7 days). No version resolver runs in the cube.

The version resolver in `src/utils/institutional_vintages.py` and the confidentiality and unknown-book rules it carries are not read by any cube step; they serve the extraction validators. The cluster part's filing deadline is `FILING_DEADLINE_DAYS` (45) in `cluster_panel.py`, and its publication day goes through `point_in_time` with the `sec13f_hr` policy of `data.yml`.

Independent closure validation is conditional for bounded code/data correctness; production adoption is separate. The 11 October live inspection found neither existing destination had verified accession columns or completion packets. Historical use therefore requires non-destructive migration and replay of original source accessions before rebuilding the institutional part and assembled cube. Manager reporting perimeters, dated CUSIP continuity, broad timing significance and delayed-source refresh remain in [TODO](../TODO.md#13f-deferred-issues-from-the-october-2026-audit). Current evidence lives in `reports/validate/2026-10-09-institutional-13f-audit/`; merge status is recorded there, not implied by this contract.

## SEC 13F cluster features

`cube_part_sec_thirteen` holds 80 float32 columns `f_ic_cluster_<id>_<feature>`. [cluster.yml](../../configs/cluster.yml) declares them (`clusters:`: seven fund clusters with 8 features each, banks 8, insurers 8, broker-dealers 4, pensions 4) and [cluster_config.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/sec_thirteen/cluster_config.py) generates the names (`column_names`), so no file lists a column by hand; the part is unmanaged. Two steps cooperate through `sec_13f_manager_details`: `build-cluster` labels the managers, `build-sec-thirteen` turns each cluster's books into ticker features. The earlier broad-institutional and superinvestor 13F columns (36) and their code no longer exist; the Dataroma roster table remains but no cube step reads it.

### Manager clusters

[StepManagerProfile.run_clustering](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_manager_profile.py) (`data_aggregate build-cluster [-F]`; helpers in [clustering.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/manager_profile/clustering.py)) writes the `cluster` column (BIGINT, nullable). Every row at or above the size floor (`fit_filters`: at least `min_positions` = 4 positions and `min_book_usd` = 20,000,000 of book, for every category alike) gets an id, and a `fund` row (`category` is `fund`) is clustered. The ten clustering features are `pct_cap_small`, `pct_cap_mid`, `pct_cap_large`, `pct_equity_us`, `pct_equity_foreign`, `pct_etf`, `active_share` and the within-quarter percentile ranks of `book_value_usd`, `hhi` and `turnover` (`turnover` is optional: it is null on a manager's first row, and such a fund takes the nearest centroid computed on the other nine features); `pct_bond` is deliberately not one (its heavy tail made k-means spend a whole cluster on a 1.1% blob of 257 convertible-arbitrage CIKs; without it the best Jaccard against the research partition rose from 0.05 to 0.775) and no return column is allowed. The standalone [ClusterTransformer](../../src/modelling/transformers/cluster.py) (equal-weight z-scores, k-means++, nearest centroid per row, no refit on `transform`) is imported from `modelling` under the [coding-standards exception](../guides/coding-standards.md).

`cluster.fit: true` refits on a seeded sample of at most `max_rows_per_quarter` eligible fund rows per report quarter over the full history, separates the fitted clusters whose centroid has `pct_etf` or `pct_equity_foreign` at or above the `exclude` shares, requires exactly `keep` (7) to remain kept (otherwise it raises and changes nothing), numbers the kept ones 0 to `keep`-1 by descending fit size, gives the dropped ones the tracked ids below, and writes [cluster_centroids.json](../../configs/cluster_centroids.json) (centroids, standardisation, `kept`, `number` and `tracked` per fitted cluster, a `version` hash). With `fit: false` (the committed and the nightly state) the step only reads that file and labels rows. Both sub-runs read their mode and date from the root run (`-F`, `--as-of`) and read `sec_13f_manager_details` bounded by the table's configured `first_filed_date` window.

Stored ids (the registry is `cluster.labels` in [cluster.yml](../../configs/cluster.yml), validated by `cluster_config`: unique ids and names, a name for every labelled id, `features` true exactly for 0 to 10, one tracked fund id per dropped cluster and every `dropped_by` an `exclude` key):

| id | name | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| -2 | `not_computable` | a fund at or above the floor with a required clustering feature null (617 rows, 0.19% of eligible rows) |
| -1 | `below_size_floor` | a row of any category with 3 or fewer positions or a book under 20,000,000 (also a row with no book at all) |
| 0 to 6 | `fund_large_cap_low_turnover`, `fund_mixed_cap_concentrated_high_turnover`, `fund_large_cap_big_book`, `fund_large_cap_low_concentration`, `fund_mixed_cap_diversified_high_turnover`, `fund_small_cap`, `fund_mid_cap_concentrated` | the kept fund clusters, numbered by descending fit size and named from the saved centroids; they build feature columns |
| 7, 8, 9, 10 | `bank`, `insurance`, `broker_dealer`, `pension` | the feature categories, from `category`; they build feature columns |
| 11, 12 | `fund_etf_heavy`, `fund_etf_mixed` | the two fund clusters dropped by `pct_etf`, numbered by centroid `pct_etf` descending (0.85, then 0.51 in the saved fit); tracked only |
| 13 | `fund_foreign_heavy` | the fund cluster dropped by `pct_equity_foreign` (0.62); tracked only |
| 14, 15 | `sovereign`, `other` | the two remaining categories; tracked only |

Only ids 0 to 10 build feature columns (the 80 columns are unchanged); 11 to 15 are stored so the managers can be followed, but a quarter's cluster members are only the managers labelled 0 to 10, so the cube step never reads the books of the tracked managers. The dropped fund clusters are numbered by the centroid value of the rule that dropped them (`pct_etf` descending, then `pct_equity_foreign` descending), never by fit size, so a refit does not swap their meaning; a refit whose dropped clusters do not match the registry's `dropped_by` keys raises and writes nothing. The size floor is applied first, to every category, so a bank with 3 positions is `-1`, not 7. A fund missing only `turnover` is labelled on the other nine features (nearest centroid, no other row involved, so the label stays point-in-time); `-2` appears only when a required feature is missing. The label of a row depends on that row and its quarter alone. Null marks a row not due yet, which waits until `period + cluster_label_delay_days` (90) days have passed and the profile has reached the row. A night labels the due null rows and writes only them (a night with none reads no centroid file and writes nothing); `-F` relabels every due row with the saved centroids and does not refit. A refit changes every historical label, and the numbers 0 to 6 mean nothing across fits, so it is followed by a full part rebuild ([run guide](../guides/run-the-pipeline.md#cluster-labels-and-the-13f-cube-part)).

Known limits of the label design (accepted, measured by the 2026-11-17 validation):

- **Accepted look-ahead on the centroids.** The centroids are fitted once on the full history (composition features only: no return, no target), so the centroid positions of an early quarter were chosen with later data in view. The labels themselves are point-in-time: a row's label comes from that row's own features, and the cube uses it one quarter later (the members of quarter `t` are the labels of `t-1`).
- **Share of `-1` and `-2`.** `-1` (below the 4-position, 20,000,000 floor) is 19.4% of the due rows of 2000, 9.4% of 2025 and 7.4% of 2026, and `-2` is at most 0.4% in any year (2 of 17,849 rows in 2026). The ETF-allocator drift shows in the tracked ids instead: in 2026 the two ETF clusters 11 and 12 hold 41.5% of the due rows (7,407 of 17,849), against 0.3% in 2000 (11 of 4,058). The previous design put those managers, the sovereign and other managers and every first-row fund into one `-1` (41% of the live labels); the ids now say which. Counts per id and year are in the validation run directory (`cl_ids_val.txt` of `reports/validate/2026-11-17-cluster-13f-features/`).
- **A manager's first row is labelled, not `-1`.** Its `turnover` is null (no previous quarter), so the fund is placed on the other nine features; 5.5% of eligible rows are labelled through this fallback.
- **The lower floor adds small books.** Funds with 4 to 9 positions and at least 20,000,000 of book are 4.6% of the floor-passing fund rows (15,010 due rows from 1,626 CIKs); they land mostly in `fund_small_cap` (34.0%) and `fund_mid_cap_concentrated` (19.5%), and 11.2% in `fund_etf_heavy`.
- **Thin clusters 9 and 10.** Broker-dealers and pensions have as few as 9 to 10 members in the thinnest early quarters, against `min_holders` of 5 and 4, so their early features are mostly null.
- **Era stability is informational.** The era ARI (fit before 2013 against 2013 on) is 0.544 on all ten raw labels of the saved fit and 0.704 on the kept view (0.004 above its 0.70 threshold; 0.718 before the 2026-11-18 refit), and an independent recomputation reads it below the threshold; see the open decision in [TODO](../TODO.md#cluster-13f-follow-ups-2026-11-18).

### Cluster features

[StepCubeSecThirteen.run](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_cube_sec_thirteen.py) plans, loads once, computes the due quarters, checks, expands to days and rewrites one window; it returns the resolved window receipt like every part.

1. Plan from the saved part with the shared Window planner (`plan_window`: the root run mode and `--as-of`, the part's `overlap` of 100 calendar days before the saved frontier, the null `warmup` inputs): the configured columns against the saved ones (a different column set stops the run before any read and says to run `data_aggregate build-sec-thirteen -F`, so a config change never starts a hidden rebuild), the labels (read bounded by the manager table's `first_filed_date` window) and the report quarters whose publication day is after the saved frontier. Quarter `t` is published on the first trading day on or after its filing deadline (`t + 45` days, rolled to a session) plus the `sec13f_hr` publication lag of [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml) (`pit.lag_days`, 7 days: `t + 52` rolled) and uses the members given by the labels of `t-1`; a missing label stops the run before any line is read. `cluster.yml` holds no start date and no lag.
2. Load once what the quarters share: the members by label period and cluster (only the managers labelled 0 to 10, the clusters that have features; the tracked ids 11 to 15 and the sentinels are neither read nor members), the CUSIP tags, the Sharadar reference and the saved price frames `mcap_pit`, `shares_outstanding_pit` and `ret` for the quarter-end market cap, shares outstanding and volatility (standard deviation of the last 250 daily returns, at least 120 observed). Every input of `data.tables.cube_part_sec_thirteen.warmup` is read in full; a finite budget is refused.
3. For each due quarter read `sec13f_hr` for its members ([cluster_lines.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/sec_thirteen/cluster_lines.py), bounded by the table's configured filing-date window), cut the raw lines at the quarter's rolled 45-day deadline (`filed_by_deadline`, so the unit-repair consensus of `clean_book` sees only filings up to that deadline), clean and classify them with the profile's `clean_book` and `classify_securities`, and build company books (`company_books`: share classes summed by CUSIP tag, originals only, each manager's rank and book total over all its common companies). Books are cached per report period, so each period is read once per run.
4. [cluster_features.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/sec_thirteen/cluster_features.py) turns the books of the quarter, the quarter before and the year before into each cluster's features per ticker. Matched managers are the cluster's members with a common book in both quarters; a ticker needs `min_holders` of them holding or exiting it, else it has no row. Nothing is zero-filled.
5. Check: when a quarter is due, the newest saved quarter is recomputed and compared with its saved rows within `consistency_tolerance`; a relabelled one stops the run, writing nothing, and says to run `-F`.
6. [cluster_panel.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/sec_thirteen/cluster_panel.py) `expand_daily` carries each quarter's values from its publication day until the next quarter's, at most `max_age_calendar_days` (100) days; a ticker missing from the newer quarter is not carried, and rows start at the first published quarter.
7. Save through `write_part` (one `replace_window` of the planned write window): a full build rewrites the whole calendar with the new schema; a normal night re-emits the saved rows of the 100-calendar-day window as saved and appends the carried days, so a night with no due quarter reads no `sec13f_hr` line.

## Insider canonical cleaning and feature contract

The insider branch of `StepCubeInstitutionals` calls one `clean_transactions` boundary before every aggregate. It keeps source values and publication/version provenance, normalizes identities/names/titles/flags/amounts, and reads the transaction-date quote from the saved `close_pit` (`period_close`). The filed price stays unless filed / `close_pit` (the same day's close, else the last one within 7 calendar days, looked up with one `merge_asof`) is above 2.0 or below 0.5 (`PRICE_BAND_HIGH`/`PRICE_BAND_LOW`; a ratio exactly on a bound is kept): then the close replaces it and the row is marked `price_repaired` and `price_estimated`. A missing or zero filed price is priced from the quote when one exists (`price_estimated`); with no quote it stays an unpriced event that keeps its shares and after-holdings for account math. A class-titled row (`Class A Common Stock`, ...) is priced and repaired against the ticker's own quote like any other row (conversion ratio 1 when no `universe_cusip` ratio line exists): class identity never renders NaN, it is approximated. In the dense legs a classed buy without a proved canonical identity takes the ticker's cap on its action session (ratio 1) and is flagged `class_approximated`; in `position_units` a class-titled row (and a row dated before the factor grid) is put on the ticker's terminal basis factor, so one unit basis holds per ticker. A short-flow secondary class with a missing conversion ratio enters at 1 (a missing class bar still flags the company-day unknown). `value` is `shares_n` x the final price; `value_usd` stays source evidence.

**Trades above 25% of shares outstanding are removed.** `_split_oversized` in [insider_quality.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/insider_quality.py) compares `shares_n` with the saved `shares_outstanding_pit` on the trade's reference day (`MAX_PCT_SHARES_OUTSTANDING`). Such a trade leaves every sum and count (it is never priced or windowed) and its direct account is listed as disputed for the pooled `purchase_pct_prior` ratio over the windows the trade would have covered; a warning lists each removed trade. On the universe: GOOGL 2016-12-27 P (6.53x shares outstanding, a typo), CMG 2006-10-06 S (50.9%), BAC 2016-09-22 P (41.2%, filed 2016-10-19) and CFG 2015-03-30 S (28.4%). A genuine block trade is removed with them by decision. The rest of a filing that carries such a trade is removed too (a filing with an impossible quantity is not trusted for its other rows; their direct accounts are disputed likewise): this takes BAC 2016-08-15 P (YNOFACE Holdings, 798.4m shares, $12.0bn, 7.8% of shares outstanding), filed in the same accession as the 41.2% row, and nothing else on the universe; sponsor and strategic sales of 10-22% of shares outstanding (TDG, AWK, BLK, HLT, ...) stay.

**Publication clock.** A Form 4 filed on day d is visible from d + the `insider_transactions.pit.lag` configured in [data.yml](../../configs/data.yml) (1) calendar day, rolled forward to the first session on or after it: the step moves `filing_date`, the original-submission date amendments point at, each ticker's first filing and the table frontier through `lag_insider_source` before cleaning, so versions, windows, positions and quiet windows read the same lagged day. A Friday filing is visible Monday. `filing_date` is EDGAR's filing date, equal to the acceptance date in every one of the 1,734 stored `acceptance_datetime` values (EDGAR rows only, acceptances up to 21:59 ET), so a post-close filing is dated the same day and the lag is what makes it usable. Schedule 13D and 13G explicitly read full stored history (`warmup: null`) because old empty-filing markers affect submitter classification; finite input budgets fail before payload loading. They use the same buffer (1 day each, read by table name): `build_ownership_feature_panel` moves each accession's filing date (marker rows included) to its publication session with the same `publication_day` roll, and shifts the source start and the completeness frontier with it, so a quiet window is observed only from the session that can see the last filing; a filing whose publication day falls beyond the last session is not yet visible. [Cleaner](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/insider_quality.py), [quote helper](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/common/quotes.py), [step](../../src/data_aggregate/transformers/step_cube_institutionals.py).

Comma/dotted and semicolon title boundaries preserve explicit roles. Ordinary corporate companions, including CFO, Sr. VP & Treasurer, VP, managing director and interim, preserve supported issuer CEO/CFO/COO roles. Explicit former/subsidiary/business-unit roles remain excluded; ambiguous issuer-name suffixes and rare unsupported companions remain unknown. Missing officer titles use bounded strictly earlier public same-owner/issuer history and explicit DEF14 name/title evidence. CIK identity has priority over strict normalized names. Current contradictions/former roles block enrichment; a parent ceo_name_proxy that could be a highest-paid-person fallback is not sufficient leadership evidence. The ordinary scoped loader explicitly includes stored issuer_cik without a registry change. `officer_role` maps a title to one of eight roles, first match winning: CEO, CFO, COO, OTHER_C_SUITE (any other chief title: CTO, CMO, CAO, CLO, CIO, CPO and similar), PRESIDENT, CHAIR, VP_LEVEL (SVP, EVP, VP, Treasurer, Controller) or `other_named_officer`. A chief title beats PRESIDENT and CHAIR, so `Chairman, President & CEO` is CEO; Vice President, Group President and `President, <unit>` are never PRESIDENT. The disjoint insider populations are assigned after this role step (see the feature contract below). [Input seam](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/inputs.py).

Insider completeness is operational metadata. When its eligible frontier extends beyond the retained price calendar, completeness is capped at that calendar's last session, preserving earlier proven quiet-window support. Economic transactions still pass through the configured publication rule and stay excluded until their own eligible session; missing completeness remains unknown.

The fifteen output fields (sixteen columns with the peer leg) keep disclosure windows. Four market-cap fields sum each eligible clean execution USD amount divided by the compatible positive action-date market cap; later output-day or filing-day capitalization cannot revalue that trade. `_action_values` in [insider_features.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/insider_features.py) selects the session, requires the saved basis and a positive `mcap_pit`, and checks dated identity.

On an authoritative trading session the exact cap cell is required. Only a non-session action date may use the immediately preceding session, at most seven calendar days earlier; an invalid selected cell never searches farther back or forward. Shares must be public by that session and use the compatible price/share basis. Future action dates stay unsupported. Later price/share observations cannot revalue an already anchored trade ratio. **Exclusion policy for the dense legs (to be confirmed by the user).** An event that carries no supported information for a feature is left out of its sums and counts instead of blanking the window: an unpriced trade (no filed price, no quote), a trade flagged `quality_uncertain`, a trade with unsupported quantity or scope, an oversized trade, and a buy without an action-date market cap or identity. A window is NaN only when such an event is active and no supported event is (`_support_sets`, `_unknown_windows` in [insider_features.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/insider_features.py)); a window with neither is the ordinary observed zero. Distinct-owner counts include unpriced trades (an owner needs no price) and ignore anonymous owners. Measured on 33 tickers (price repair, cap and lag applied, before the policy): the NaN rate of `buy_value_mcap_{60,120,180}d` went 2.44/3.92/5.00% to 2.31/3.52/4.28% of live cells; the remainder is a buy with no action-date cap, almost always a class-titled row whose class identity is not proved (KMI `Class P`, ERIE `Class A`, DELL `Class V`). Where an excluded event and a value coexist (0.13/0.40/0.72% of live cells at 60/120/180d) the excluded dollar share has median 0.17/0.17/0.22 and exceeds 0.5 in 10/23/26% of those cells, so such a sum can understate. Confirmed quiet zeros do not require today's cap.

**2026-11-13 redesign: seventeen insider features, three populations.** Every row gets one population from its cleaned `officer_title` and flags (`TITLE_POPULATIONS` and `_assign_population` in [insider_quality.py](../../src/data_aggregate/utils/institutionals/insider_quality.py)), first match wins: C_SUITE (any chief title, founder, EVP, any executive title, chair/chairman/vice chair, President and Group President; `Vice President` and `Group Vice President` are not); then MANAGEMENT (`is_director == 1`, or SVP/VP, general counsel, treasurer/controller/secretary, strategy, managing director, any other officer title); then TEN_PCT (`is_ten_pct_owner == 1`); else OTHER. An officer whose title is blank or `See Remarks` after the prior-evidence fill is OTHER: it counts only in the all-insider features and never blanks a population feature. Former/retired titles never match. OTHER rows (56% of purchase dollars) appear only in all-insider features. Features after the 2026-11-14 merge below: `buy_value_mcap_{60,120,180}d` plus the merged `cm_buy_mcap_180d`; `purchase_pct_prior_{60,120}d`; `distinct_buyers_120d`, `distinct_sellers_120d`, `buyer_breadth_120d` (buyers over distinct P-or-S insiders, NaN when nobody traded); `net_buy_ratio_{60,120,180}d`. The population split of the buy legs and of the 180d purchase ratios was replaced the same week (next paragraph).

Counts stay distinct counts and net-buy ratio remains a dollar ratio at three horizons. `purchase_pct_prior_<w>d` is the net P minus S shares of the supported direct accounts that traded in the window, over those accounts' pooled holdings just before their first window trade, summed per ticker over every share class. An account is one owner's direct holdings line of one class, chained through `shares_owned_after`. An account whose chain cannot be established (missing balance, broken chain, other-flow blocker, interleaved expired trade, units unknown across days, disputed filing) is left out of numerator, denominator and counts, so one bad account never blanks the ticker; the cell is NaN only when no supported account remains, the pooled prior is not positive, or the supported accounts' unit bases differ (`bases == 1`, keyed on `(ticker, unit_basis)`). The ratio then measures the accounts we can read: among the cells the old rule blanked, the left-out accounts were a median 80-87% of the traded shares. The ratio has no lower bound of -1: a seller who gained shares inside the window (grants, conversion, vesting) can sell more than the balance one window earlier, and the live part holds cells down to about -20 (3,245 at 60d, 9,046 at 120d below -1); the downstream 1/99 winsorisation bounds it, and there is no validate bound above. Positions above 1e12 shares (a filer's typo that would wrap the integer micro-share sums) leave the account out. Disputed accounts are excluded for their whole history, not only for the window of the disputed trade. A ticker with no unit basis converts at the configured factor 1.0 in every path (short volume, FTD, shares, positions): level ratios against the tape (turnover, `ic_ftd_to_adv20`, short-volume ratio) are therefore unreliable for an ADR whose quote unit differs from the filing unit. Each lot keeps its original disclosure expiry; amendments apply prospectively; pooled sums use exact integer micro-shares. Rows that are not an identified direct account (indirect, joint, no owner CIK, unknown class) are not pooled. No owner-surprise rank, cluster gate or 10b5-1 split remains.
